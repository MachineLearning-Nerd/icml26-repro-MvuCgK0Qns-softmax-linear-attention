# Limitations and deviations

- The theorem is qualitative: neither the paper nor this certificate provides
  an explicit numerical `L(epsilon)`.
- The certificate treats “loss” as bounded below, as required for the
  non-increasing risk limit used in the paper's proof.
- A proof-display `beta_L` subscript is resolved to the fixed `beta_infinity`
  actually established and used in Lemma D.1 equations (22)-(24).
- No finite training curve is presented as proof of the universal statement.
