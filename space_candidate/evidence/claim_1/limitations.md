# Limitations and deviations

- The result falsifies the proposition as written, not a charitable repaired
  statement with an added lower bound on `L`.
- It does not estimate the unspecified constants or test the asymptotic rate.
- No Monte Carlo approximation is used; the contradiction is exact.
- The witness is one-dimensional, which is sufficient for a universal claim
  stated for general `d` and is not presented as a full-scale empirical run.
